FAQ

Common Questions.

How does the Delegate feature work?
X's native Delegate feature lets you authorize a trusted person to post on your account without sharing your password. Everything goes through X's official system, no third-party tools, no account risk. You keep full control and can revoke access any time.
How do I know it won't sound like AI or a bot?
Because it isn't. Every reply is written by a human who has studied your posts, your takes, and how you actually talk. In the first week we send drafts for your review, most clients can't tell the difference from their own writing. If a reply ever feels off, we rewrite it, no questions asked.
How do you capture my voice?
15-minute call. We learn how you think, what you believe, and how you naturally speak on CT. We study your past posts and takes right after, so by day one the replies already sound exactly like you.
What accounts do you reply to?
We identify the top 20-30 accounts your ideal audience follows, VCs, founders, analysts, KOLs in your niche. Every reply is strategically placed, not random. Quality of placement matters more than volume.
How quickly will I see results?
Most clients see a measurable uptick in profile visits and follower growth within 2-3 weeks. Deal flow and inbound DMs typically start appearing in weeks 4-6 as the presence compounds over time.
What if I want to pause or cancel?
No lock-in. Cancel anytime with 7 days notice. We don't believe in trapping clients, we believe in results that make you want to stay.
Can I see replies before they go live?
Yes. We can set up a review workflow where you approve replies before they post. Most clients prefer letting us run it directly after the first week, once trust is established. Your call, always.
