FAQ

Common questions.

How do I know it won't sound like AI or a bot?
Because it isn't. Every reply is written by a human who has studied your posts, your takes, and how you actually talk. In the first week we send drafts for your review, so you can check the voice before anything runs on its own. If a reply ever feels off, we rewrite it, no questions asked.
Is this safe for my account?
Every reply is written and posted by a person, through X’s own Delegate feature. No bots, no automation tools, no engagement pods. You keep full control of the account and can remove our access at any time.
How does the Delegate feature work?
X's native Delegate feature lets you authorize a trusted person to post on your account without sharing your password. Everything goes through X's official system, no third-party tools, no account risk. You keep full control and can revoke access any time.
How many replies a day?
It’s scoped to your goals on the call. In the client reports above, the personal brand ran about 30 replies a day and Multipli’s intern account about 50 a day.
Can I see replies before they go live?
Yes. We can set up a review workflow where you approve replies before they post. Once the voice is dialed in after week one, you can switch to hands-off. Your call, always.
How do you capture my voice?
We start with a 30-minute call to understand what you need. Then we study your posts, opinions, humour, phrasing, and how you respond to people to map your voice and tonality. From there, we build a voice profile your writer uses from day one, so the replies already sound exactly like you.
Do you work with project accounts or only founders?
Both. We run replies for project accounts, founder accounts, and branded intern accounts like Multipli’s. Many teams start with one account and add the founder later.
What accounts do you reply to?
We identify the top 20-50 accounts your ideal audience follows, VCs, founders, analysts, KOLs in your niche. Every reply is strategically placed, not random. Quality of placement matters more than volume.
How quickly will I see results?
In client accounts so far, impressions and profile visits moved within the first one to three weeks; the reports above show the exact numbers. Inbound DMs and deals take longer and aren't guaranteed, so we report what moves every Sunday.
What if I want to pause or cancel?
No lock-in. Cancel anytime with 7 days notice. We don't believe in trapping clients, we believe in results that make you want to stay.
